The fastest review trick I know is also the oldest: read the diff out loud, top to bottom, as if explaining it to someone with no context.

When you are forced to narrate, the gaps surface. The function named `validate` that never validates. The `catch` block that swallows the error. The branch that is unreachable because of the guard two lines up.

## Why it works

Silent review is pattern matching. Spoken review is a simulation. Your brain cannot gloss over a step it has to verbalize.

Do it solo before you post the PR. You will close more comments yourself than the reviewer ever would.
